# Brief: Puzzle Words (letter puzzle blending, kindergarten)
1. Topic: teach a kindergarten child to blend sounds with letter puzzle pieces (IES "Blending with Letter Puzzles (Level 3)"): snap the pieces, say each sound, slide the sounds together, read the word. 13 words: runs lamp nest soft past send milk list film self mist must lump.
2. Look: simple flat 2D, big tiles, single-story letters (Andika font), soft pastel toy colors. Teacher is MICA in 2D (same face: white head, dark screen, teal band, blush).
3. Characters: MICA (teacher) only. No distractions.
4. Format: explainer for a child. I do (lamp) -> we do (nest) -> you do (milk) -> word train (10 words) -> recap.
5. Voices: MICA = Piper "amy" voice, slowed for kids. Single sounds made straight from phonemes.
6. Length: about 4 minutes.
7. (reaction only) n/a
8. Include: pauses so the child can answer. Vowel tiles pink, consonant tiles blue. Leave out: names, money, anything scary.
Camera: steady, no moves, on purpose (a child must see the letters stay still). Size 9:16.
