# Brief: "Heart Calm" (Quran lesson, Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:28)
1. Story: Dalia grew up Muslim and knows all the words, but it feels like background noise. Amina shows her one verse (13:28), breaks down 3 Arabic words, then gives 3 small daily actions to practice it.
2. Look: emotional robots
3. Characters: Dalia (learning), Amina (wise friend, hijab)
4. Format: story (teacher + student) with simple info cards (Arabic words + meaning)
5. Voices: their own voices (Chatterbox)
6. Length: about 2 minutes
7. (not a reaction video)
8. Include / avoid: ARABIC QURAN ONLY, NO HADITH (no "the Prophet said", no hadith-based wording). Reading level: 5th grade. Every point ends in an action she can do. Never record Arabic recitation with the TTS (only the written verse is shown). No music on purpose (only soft whooshes and room sound). No real family names on screen.
Verses used (text copied from the Uthmani text, api.alquran.cloud): 13:28 (full), 2:152 (first part), 3:191 (a middle part).
Simple English meanings are written by us, not a word for word translation.
