# Cast: humans only
Use `kit.mkHuman` and `kit.PEOPLE` (characters/robots.js). Full bodies with legs. Cute faces: dot eyes, blush, simple mouth.
- Pick people who match the story: vary skin tone, hair, age, glasses, hijab. Use at least 2 clearly different looks. Dalia herself = `dalia` (long dark hair, pink top).
- Give each person a different top color so viewers can tell them apart at a glance.
- Names for voices: DALIA, AMINA, MARCUS, LILY, SOFIA, GRANDPA, KID_BOY, KID_GIRL, NARRATOR.
- Acting: `animateBot(person, t, { speaking, amp, emo, wave, walk, sit })`. Emotions: happy excited deadpan sweet stern tired dramatic worried shock smug laugh.
- Humans have no antenna or screen. Show feeling with the face, head tilt, arms, a hop, and props (mug, laptop, phone).
- No robots on screen.
