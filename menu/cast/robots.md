# Cast: robots only
Use `kit.mkBot(key)` with the robot cast: MICA, HOOKY, CHARGEY, DRAFTY, LISTER, LOGBOT (robots) and LEFTY, KRAFT, DUSTY, DYMO, STATUS (printer robots).
- Full bodies with legs. Keep each robot's own prop and accent color.
- Their personalities (from the podcast): Mica the warm host, Lefty eager, Kraft the stickler, Dusty the tired veteran, Dymo sweet and tiny, Status the dashboard that keeps checking in, Hooky dramatic, Chargey serious, Drafty gentle, Lister ranks everything, Logbot deadpan.
- No humans on screen.
