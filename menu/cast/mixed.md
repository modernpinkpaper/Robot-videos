# Cast: humans and robots together
Mix `kit.mkHuman` people with `kit.mkBot` robots in the same scene. Same light rig, same scale (both are about 7 units tall).
- Give the robot a clear job in the scene (helper, coworker, narrator, the one who says the obvious thing).
- Keep the humans as the heart of the story when the topic is about people.
- Voices: humans and robots both use voices_cfg names; robots sound slightly more synthetic (already tuned).
