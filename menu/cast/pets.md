# Add pets and animals (works with any cast)
Use `kit.mkPet('dog'|'cat'|'bunny'|'cow'|'sheep'|'chicken'|'bird'|'pig', {...})` and `kit.animatePet(pet, t, { walk, happy, sniff })`.
Pets have no voices: show reactions with tail wag, hop, head tilt, and little sound effects. Add props from `kit.mkProp` (bone, fish, ball, balloon...).
