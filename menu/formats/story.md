# Format: story (storyline)
A plot with a beginning, a problem, a turn and an ending. 5 to 8 scenes, 60 to 90 seconds.
- Structure: HOOK (first 3 seconds, a question or a strange moment) -> SETUP -> PROBLEM -> TURN -> ENDING with a feeling.
- A narrator is optional. Lines are short (under 12 words). Captions on screen for every line.
- Each scene is a different place (set) with its own light.
- Camera: follow `videos/no_lies/` exactly. Open with an aerial or wide move. 3 to 5 shots per scene. Whip-pan between scenes. Pull back at the end.
- Shot recipe per scene: wide establish -> push in on speaker -> close-up reaction -> special move (orbit, low angle, pan) -> wide.

## Acting (do not skip: the faces and the body ARE the story)
- Write a beat for every important line: what the face does, what the body does, where the character looks. Put them in a `perform.js` next to `plan.py` (see `videos/quran_calm/perform.js`).
- Use the shared automatic layer for everything else: `characters/expressive/behavior.js` (listeners nod, tilt, lean and look at the speaker; speakers shift and nod at sentence ends).
- Give each main character a posture arc across the story (`slump`, `lean`, `tilt`): heavy at the start, light at the end, or the other way round.
- Serious or sad moments are still and soft. Funny moments get a laugh with the body. A look to the camera only after something absurd or too true.
- Check with `node tools/movement_report.js videos/<name>` and the fit sheet, then do the rule audit (`menu/QUALITY.md` step 6).
