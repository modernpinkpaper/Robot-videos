# Format: skit (sketch)
One short funny scene, 2 or 3 characters, 45 to 75 seconds. Comedy through contrast: one wants something, the other blocks it.
- Structure: SETUP (who, where, what they want) -> ESCALATION (it gets worse, 3 beats, each bigger) -> PUNCHLINE -> TAG (a tiny extra joke after the laugh).
- Keep it to 1 or 2 places. Props matter (laptop, mug, phone, sign, desk, chair).
- Funny comes from specifics and reactions: show the reaction face in close-up after every joke.
- Lines are fast and short. Overlap the energy: next line starts quickly (gap 0.1 to 0.3 s).
- Camera: shot-reverse-shot between the two speakers (closeUp on A, closeUp on B), a push in on the escalation, a dolly zoom or zoomLens on the worst moment, a wide for the punchline, a pull back for the tag. Cut every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds.
- Work-skit example beats (coworker, hard day): morning, the coworker takes your idea; the printer jams; the coworker says "quick question" 5 times; end of day, they bring you coffee. Keep it kind: no real names, no real company.
