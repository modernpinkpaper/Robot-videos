# Format: talking directly to the camera
One character speaks straight to the viewer like a creator, 30 to 45 seconds.
- Structure: HOOK line in the first 2 seconds ("Nobody tells you this about...") -> 3 short points or one short story -> a clear last line + question to the viewer.
- The character faces the camera (ry = 0) and keeps eye contact. Use `wave`, head nods and small gestures.
- Background: one simple set (room, desk, street) that matches the topic. Props behind them.
- Camera: never static. Slow push in, then a zoomLens or closeUp on key words, small whip cut to a slightly different angle every 2 to 3 seconds (jump cuts), pull back at the end. Add captions with big words highlighted.
- Cutaways are allowed: a quick 1.5 second shot of a prop or a second character acting out what is said.
