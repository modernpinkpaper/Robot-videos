# Format: explainer / how-to
A teacher character explains something step by step with info cards, 2 to 3 minutes (or 45 to 60 s short).
- Structure: HOOK question -> why it matters -> 3 to 6 steps (one card each) -> recap -> one action to try today.
- Use info cards like the podcast (title + 2 to 4 short bullets) shown beside the speaker. See formats/podcast/anim/index_pod3.html for the card style.
- A second character can ask the simple question the viewer is thinking.
- Camera: push in on the teacher, cut to the card, orbit or crane for the recap.
